## Corrections to Corpus tags

These occurrences carry an adjudicated correction or a documented source/analysis disagreement. The lessons follow the recorded contextual analysis; the raw Corpus data itself is unchanged.

- **18:1:11** عِوَجَاۜ: QAC tags عِوَجَا nominative, but it ends in fatha + alif (tanwin al-fath, read ʿiwajā at Hafs' saktah) and is the object of jussive يَجْعَل: accusative.
- **18:12:7** لِمَا: QAC tags the stem of لِمَا REL, deriving pronoun, but the primary 18:12 verse i‘rab explicitly calls ما مصدرية. Follow the selected masdar-particle analysis: lam governs the interpreted masdar, attached to an implied hal of amadan. The effective app word type is particle; preserve raw QAC tags and exact Arabic.
- **18:13:10** وَزِدْنَـٰهُمْ: QAC tags وَزِدْنَـٰهُمْ PERF PASS with نَا as na'ib al-fa'il. It is active: نَا is the subject (We increased them), هُمْ the first object and هُدًى the second object (QAC's own 18:13 note: «هدى» مفعول ثانٍ; all translations active). The 1P perfect زِدْنَا is spelled the same in both voices.
- **18:18:6** ذَاتَ: QAC tags this word NOM, contradicting the vocalised form and its role. ذَاتَ ٱلْيَمِينِ is an adverb of place (ظرفِ مکان) of نُقَلِّبُهُمْ, accusative with fatha.
- **18:18:8** وَذَاتَ: QAC tags this word NOM, contradicting the vocalised form and its role. وَذَاتَ ٱلشِّمَالِ is coordinated with the adverb ذَاتَ ٱلْيَمِينِ: accusative with fatha.
- **18:18:12** ذِرَاعَيْهِ: QAC tags this word NOM, contradicting the vocalised form and its role. ذِرَاعَيْهِ is the object of the active participle بَـٰسِطٌ; the dual ya ending marks the accusative.
- **18:20:3** يَظْهَرُوا۟: QAC tags يَظْهَرُوا۟ subjunctive; it is the jussive verb of the conditional إِنْ (its nun dropped for jazm), answered by jussive يَرْجُمُوكُمْ.
- **18:20:5** يَرْجُمُوكُمْ: QAC tags يَرْجُمُوكُمْ subjunctive, but no particle of nasb precedes it; it is the jussive answer (jawab al-shart) of the conditional إِن يَظْهَرُوا۟, and the dropped nun is the sign of jazm.
